## 4. Content Pillars (what the channel is "about")

1. **Hidden dangers** — hidden cameras, scams, everyday safety (your first video ✅)
2. **"What happens if..."** — body, physics, survival scenarios
3. **How things secretly work** — devices, tricks, systems around you
4. **Facts that sound fake** — short, shocking, shareable

Stay inside these 4 lanes for the first 20 videos so the algorithm learns your audience.

## 5. Branding

- **Logo / profile pic:** Bold single letter **"M"** or a stylized eye 👁 (fits the
  "see the hidden" theme). Flat 3D render, one accent color on dark background.
- **Colors:** Dark navy `#0D1B2A` + electric red `#FF3B3B` accent + white text.
  (Red = danger/curiosity — matches the hidden-camera niche.)
- **Banner text:** "Animated videos about the hidden side of everything — new every week"
- **Thumbnail style (keep consistent):** one big 3D-rendered object, red arrow or
  circle, 3–5 word text max, shocked context. Same font every time (Montserrat ExtraBold).
