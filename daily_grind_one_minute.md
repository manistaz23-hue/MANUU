# "The Daily Grind" — 1-Minute Script (opens with the "when a man..." hook)

**Runtime target:** ~60 seconds
**Delivery:** calm hook → machine-gun list → hard slowdown at "Now — a baby."
→ dead serious close.
**Note:** written platform-clean so TikTok/Reels won't mute it; profanity can
be added back for uncensored platforms.

---

When a man tells you, "I wanna get you pregnant" — before you hear romance,
I need you to think about one average day of your life.

Every single day — EVERY day — you gotta wake up and maintain a whole human
being: you. You gotta eat. Three times. You gotta shower. Brush your teeth.
Floss. Mouthwash. Work out. Get your fiber. Get your protein. Check on your
gut health. Answer calls, answer texts, book appointments, renew passports.
Hydrate, meditate, do yoga, think happy thoughts — maybe get into Buddhism.
Every single day you gotta choose to exist. Choose to live. That's ONE life.
Yours. And you're barely keeping up.

[beat]

Now — a baby.

A whole extra human being who can't do ANY of that for themselves. You do
all of it — twice. The feeding, the washing, the appointments, the three
a.m. crying. Eighteen years. Minimum. No days off. No unsubscribe button.

So "I wanna get you pregnant" really means: "Let's double the hardest job
you already have. Forever."

A child is not a vibe. It's the most permanent decision of your life. Make
it for the wrong reason, at the wrong time, with the wrong person — and it's
the one mistake you can never take back.
