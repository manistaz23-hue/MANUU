# "The Daily Grind" — 1-Minute Script (Why a Child Is the Worst Mistake You Can Make Unready)

**Runtime target:** ~60 seconds
**Delivery:** relentless machine-gun list for the first half, then slow down
hard at "Now — a baby." and stay dead serious to the end.
**Note:** written platform-clean so TikTok/Reels won't mute it; profanity can
be added back for uncensored platforms.

---

Every single day — EVERY day — you gotta wake up and maintain a whole human
being: you. You gotta eat. Three times. Breakfast, lunch, dinner. You gotta
shower. Brush your teeth. Floss. Mouthwash. You gotta work out. Get your
fiber. Get your protein. Check on your gut health. You gotta answer calls,
answer texts, book appointments, renew passports. You gotta hydrate,
meditate, do yoga, think happy thoughts — maybe get into Buddhism. Every
single day you gotta choose to exist. Choose to live. That's ONE life.
Yours. And you're barely keeping up.

[beat]

Now — a baby.

A whole extra human being. Who can't do ANY of that for themselves. You do
all of it — twice. The feeding, the washing, the doctor's appointments, the
three a.m. crying. For eighteen years. Minimum. No days off. No unsubscribe
button.

So when somebody casually says "let's have a kid" like they're ordering
takeout — hear what they're really saying: "Let's double the hardest job
you already have. Forever."

A child is not a vibe. It's the most permanent decision of your life. Make
it for the wrong reason, at the wrong time, with the wrong person — and it's
not just a mistake. It's the one mistake you can never take back.
