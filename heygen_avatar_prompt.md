## Avatar & Scene Prompt

Create a 60-second vertical (9:16) talking-head video for TikTok/Reels/Shorts.

**Avatar:** A confident Black woman in her late 20s to early 30s, natural
curly hair or neat braids, gold hoop earrings, wearing a stylish but casual
outfit (fitted blazer over a plain top). She looks directly into the camera
the entire time, like she's giving her best friend a reality check.

**Setting:** Modern, softly lit living room or creator-studio corner — warm
lamp light, a couch or bookshelf slightly blurred in the background. Framing
is chest-up, centered, phone-camera intimacy — it should feel like a
front-facing camera rant, not a corporate presentation.

**Performance direction (two distinct halves):**

1. **0:00–0:22 — Theatrical & fast.** High energy, animated, storytelling
   mode: raised eyebrows, hand gestures, head tilts, sarcastic smirks. She's
   performing the "dramatic version" like stand-up comedy — big, exaggerated,
   almost gossiping.
2. **0:22–0:60 — Dead serious & slower.** At "Sounds crazy? Good." she drops
   the act completely: face settles, gestures become minimal and deliberate,
   voice slows down, she leans slightly toward the camera. Calm, direct,
   almost stern — like a mentor telling hard truth. Hold eye contact.
   On the final line, a small pause before "That's the hard part," then a
   slow single nod. End on a steady, unsmiling look into the lens.

**Style notes:** No background music louder than -25dB (or none at all — the
voice carries it). Natural micro-pauses at the em-dashes. Auto-captions ON,
word-by-word, bold white with black outline, centered lower third.

## Script (paste into the script/voice field)

When a man tells you, "I wanna get you pregnant" — let me tell you what your
brain should hear.

First, the dramatic version: he wants to unalive you, annihilate your whole
family, drain every bank account — even the secret one — steal your Social
Security, become you, and live your whole life ruining everything you built.
Then revive you from the dead, just to do it all over again.

[beat — drop the energy here]

Sounds crazy? Good. Because here's the real version, and it's just as
serious: "I want to permanently change your life."

That's what pregnancy is. It's not nine months — it's your body, your
health, your career, your money, your freedom. There is no such thing as
"just" a baby.

So don't hear romance first. Hear responsibility. Hear sleepless nights,
medical bills, years of sacrifice — a future that can never be undone.

That's why the conversation about having children should never happen just
because the moment feels right. It should happen because both people
understand exactly what they're signing up for — the responsibility, the
support, the consequences. If he's not ready to carry all of that with you,
he's not ready to ask.

[pause]

Because words are easy. Living with the outcome? That's the hard part.
