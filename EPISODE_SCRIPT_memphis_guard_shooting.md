## SCRIPT

> not by police… by **soldiers**.

**[LOWER THIRD: "Memphis, TN — July 5, 2026"]**

- *Credit on screen:* **"Footage: FOX13 Memphis"**

**[VO OVER CLIP]**

> Four a.m., downtown Memphis. Police respond to shots fired and spot
> an armed man who takes off running.

### THE FACTS — 0:20–0:38 [YOU — ON CAMERA]

> Chasing him: Memphis police — **and Tennessee National Guard soldiers**,
> deployed there since October under a federal crime task force.
> Two soldiers opened fire. Twenty-year-old Tyrin Johnson died at the scene.

**[LOWER THIRD: "Tyrin Johnson, 20 — died at the scene (TBI)"]**

### WHY IT MATTERS — 0:38–0:52 [YOU — ON CAMERA, slower]

> This appears to be the **first fatal shooting** by Guard troops on this deployment.
> Soldiers are trained for combat — not policing. So whose rules applied
> on that street? Right now… nobody can say.

### OUTRO — 0:52–1:00 [YOU — ON CAMERA]

> The state bureau of investigation is on it, and lawmakers want answers.
> Follow for the update when the footage drops.
