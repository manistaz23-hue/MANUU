# SHOW UP — "A Generous Offer"

**1-minute cut · ~56.6s nominal · 53 lines/min**

> POV: You complained to HR about your salary… and your manager comes back with 3%.

## Cast & anchors

| | Design | Delivery | Voice ref |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SARAH** (manager) | Janet design — puffy side-bunches, headband, wide expressive mouth | Jovial → brittle → shouting. Never stops smiling until "Just take it." | PTO Meme Voice<br>`fdd8dca2-b602-417f-8c34-0a4cf186fe1d` (element) |
| **NIA** (agent) | Veronica design — ponytail, striped shirt, heavy-lidded deadpan | Flat, fast, zero effort. Her "No" costs her nothing, and that's the joke. | Unhinged Survival Tactics Voice<br>`6e04b631-b469-4c36-b1a5-a6a39e7ff24c` (element) |

**Setting.** Call center floor. Nia is working at her station. Sarah walks over with a folder and an enormous smile.

**Pacing rule — this is the whole scene.** Nia's replies land *on top of* Sarah's line ends, never after a think. Sarah is the only one who pauses. The scene accelerates: 2-second lines at the top, quarter-second lines by the end. If a take feels comfortable, it's too slow.

**Blocking rule.** The folder is the tell. Held up like evidence through the pitch, drops to her side on "Just take it," gripped two-handed by "FINE! FIVE!" Nia never stops typing — including on the last line.

## Script

**SARAH:** Nia! I talked to HR about your complaint.

**NIA:** Okay.

**SARAH:** I wanted you to hear it from me.

**NIA:** Hear what?

**SARAH:** Fantastic news.

**NIA:** How much?

**SARAH:** **Three percent!**

*[Nia just stares at her.]*

**NIA:** …That's the fantastic news?

**SARAH:** Most people got two!

**NIA:** Inflation was five.

**SARAH:** But it's still a raise.

**NIA:** So I'm losing money with enthusiasm.

**SARAH:** That's not how I'd frame it.

**NIA:** How would you frame it?

**SARAH:** …Growth.

**NIA:** Growth.

**SARAH:** I personally fought for this.

**NIA:** Against who?

**SARAH:** HR.

**NIA:** And?

**SARAH:** They said you're fairly paid.

**NIA:** So you lost.

**SARAH:** I won three percent!

**NIA:** Can I decline?

**SARAH:** Why would you decline money?

**NIA:** It's not money. It's less money.

**SARAH:** This is a good outcome.

**NIA:** For who?

**SARAH:** It is not a pay cut!

**NIA:** Costs up five. Pay up three.

**SARAH:** Don't make this math.

**NIA:** You made it math.

**SARAH:** Just take it.

**NIA:** No.

**SARAH:** Please?

**NIA:** No.

**SARAH:** It'll look bad.

**NIA:** Then HR can explain.

**SARAH:** …You'd do that?

**NIA:** I'd enjoy it.

*[Sarah's smile disappears.]*

**SARAH:** Four percent.

**NIA:** No.

**SARAH:** Four and a quarter.

**NIA:** No.

**SARAH:** Four and a half.

**NIA:** No.

**SARAH:** NIA. TAKE THE RAISE.

**NIA:** Not at four and a half.

**SARAH:** **FINE! FIVE!**

*[Silence.]*

**NIA:** Now we're negotiating.

*[Sarah realizes she just exposed herself.]*

**CUT.**
