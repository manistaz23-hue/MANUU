# 002 — The Burglar Who Picked the Wrong Grandma

**Story:** Willie Murphy, 82, Rochester NY, November 21 2019, ~11pm.
**Format:** 60s · 6 blocks × 10s · 9:16 vertical · faceless · English · no subtitles.
**Sources:** washingtonpost.com/sports/2019/11/25 · abcnews.com (id 67289683) · spectrumlocalnews.com 2019/11/26

**Verified facts:** Man pounded on her door claiming to be sick, begging for an
ambulance. She called police, refused to open. He broke through the door. House
dark; she was an award-winning WNPF senior powerlifter (2014 lifter of the year)
who deadlifts 225 lbs. She broke a table over him, poured baby shampoo in his
eyes while he was down, hit him with a broom. Police arrived; he was taken away
in an ambulance — the thing he'd asked for. She posed for selfies with first
responders. Her quote: "I'm alone and I'm old, but guess what? I'm TOUGH."

Block 3
She called for help but refused to open the door. Furious, he smashed straight through it seconds later.

Block 4

Block 5
Hiding in the dark, she broke a whole table over him, poured shampoo in his eyes, then grabbed a broom.

Block 6
