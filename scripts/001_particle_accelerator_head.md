## Narration (voiced per block, seed_audio, same voice)

Block 1
In nineteen seventy-eight, a Soviet scientist accidentally put his head inside a running particle accelerator. Somehow, he lived to tell the story.

Block 2
Anatoli Bugorski was leaning in to check a faulty part inside the Soviet Union's largest accelerator, when every safety system silently failed at once.

Block 3
A beam of protons moving at nearly the speed of light shot straight through the back of his skull, and came out beside his nose.

Block 4
He saw a flash brighter than a thousand suns, but felt no pain at all. The beam carried hundreds of times the lethal dose of radiation.

Block 5
It should have killed him, but it burned only a pencil-thin tunnel. His skin peeled, half his face froze, and one ear went silent forever.

Block 6
Doctors watched him, waiting for him to die. Instead, he finished his doctorate and went back to work. And the frozen half of his face never aged.
