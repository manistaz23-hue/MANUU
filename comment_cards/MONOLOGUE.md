# "THE COMMENT SECTION" — full spoken-word monologue (~2.75 min)

Built ONLY from the original post's comment set (cards 01-14). One continuous
take. Presenter solo at the mic, calm and deliberate (~130 wpm, 0.95x).
Comment cards from comment_cards/out/ dangle into frame on strings, one group
per movement, swaying gently; yanked up between movements. On "that's the
pinned comment," card_06 drops down alone and hangs through the final
movement.

Card map: screams = 02/03/04, the drop = 05, receipts = 07/09, performances =
08/12, quiet ones = 10/11, pinned = 06, gut = 13, closer = series catchphrase.

---

Somebody asked the internet a simple question: give me your most unhinged
survival hacks. Don't say "always keep pepper spray." Tell me what
unexpectedly saved your life. And a hundred thousand women answered. This is
what the comment section knows.

It knows you never scream "help." Nobody runs toward someone else's problem.
You scream FIRE — because everybody runs toward a spectacle. It knows you
scream "MOM" — because every mother within a mile will turn her head. It knows
you never yell "leave me alone" — strangers hear a lovers' quarrel and keep
walking. You yell "I DON'T KNOW YOU"... and a sidewalk full of strangers
becomes a room full of witnesses.

It knows that if he grabs you, you don't fight upward. You drop. Go limp. Dead
weight takes both of his hands... and your mouth still works. A police officer
taught somebody that. She never forgot it.

The comment section keeps receipts. A bat in the car — with a glove and a
ball, because a bat alone is premeditated, but the whole set is just practice.
A decoy wallet with five fake dollars, ready to hand over. Tip money riding
home in a to-go box... because nobody robs leftovers.

And when it has to, it performs. It talks to the cat like a roommate — loud,
through the door: "I'M HOME, BABE. DON'T WAIT UP." Now the whole hallway
believes in a man who doesn't exist. And when a stranger creeps too close, it
walks up to the nearest auntie and says "MOM — this guy is bothering me."
Congratulations. You've been adopted.

Some of it is quieter. A secret fund he will never find. A fake name for
strangers — because you don't owe the truth to anyone you don't know. And from
a real 9-1-1 operator, one for free: if you don't know where you are — mile
markers, exit signs, store names. That's how they find you.

Nobody teaches this in school. You think it's just women being dramatic. You
think it's just another comment section. But every comment is a lesson
somebody paid for — posted by a woman who lived long enough to type it.

So here's what it all comes down to. Loud works. Weird works. Disgusting
works. Spit. Slobber. Bark. Sing off-key about nothing. You can wash every bit
of it off later... alive. That's not my line. That's the pinned comment.

And trust your gut. It's the one alarm that never needs charging. If it feels
off... it is off.

Because out here, pretty is optional. Polite is optional. Alive... is the
whole assignment. Stay sexy. Stay feral. Stay alive.

opens part two.
