Okay. This might be the most insightful comment section on the internet.
Somebody asked women for their most unhinged survival hacks — and I mean
unhinged. What they got back was a survival guide. This is what the comment
section knows.

It knows that if a woman gets a male Uber driver and he asks about her day,
she's suddenly thrilled — because she just got her concealed-carry permit. And
if he asks about her job? She's not at liberty to discuss that with the
general public. Iconic... really. Because you don't owe the truth to anyone
you don't know. A fake name for strangers. A boyfriend who doesn't exist. A
cat she talks to through the door — "I'M HOME, BABE" — loud enough that the
whole hallway believes in a man who was never born. It's literally okay to lie
to strangers. That's not deceit. That's armor.

And it knows the hard rules. The ones that came from a cop who visited
somebody's high school and told the girls: you have a better chance of
surviving a gunshot than a man who takes you to a second location. Never let
him force you into the car. Not even at gunpoint. It knows you never scream
"help" — nobody runs toward someone else's problem. You scream FIRE, because
everybody runs toward a spectacle. You scream "MOM," because every mother
within a mile will turn her head. And if he grabs you? You don't fight upward.
You drop. Go limp. Dead weight takes both of his hands... and your mouth still
works.

It teaches the children, too: a real adult will never ask a child for help.
They don't need a child to find a lost dog... or whatever. Adults ask adults.
Teach them young.

The comment section keeps receipts. A bat in the car — with a glove and a
ball, because a bat alone is premeditated, but the whole set is just practice.
A decoy wallet with five fake dollars. Tip money riding home in a to-go box,
because nobody robs leftovers. A secret fund he will never find. And from a
real 9-1-1 operator, one for free: if you don't know where you are — mile
markers, exit signs, store names. That's how they find you. And when a
stranger creeps too close, walk up to the nearest auntie and say "MOM — this
guy is bothering me." Congratulations. You've been adopted.

And when you feel eyes on you — when you sense him staring — stop. Stare back.
No smile. No fear. No emotion. Now YOU know what HE looks like... and you just
became less of a target. Stay aware.

Nobody teaches this in school. You think it's just women being dramatic. You
think it's just another comment section. But every comment is a lesson
somebody paid for — posted by a woman who lived long enough to type it.

So here's what it all comes down to. Loud works. Weird works. Disgusting works
— spit, slobber, bark, sing off-key about nothing. You can wash every bit of
it off later... alive. That's not my line. That's the pinned comment.

And the last one. The one that got me. Women were given a heightened sense of
intuition to offset the imbalance of physical strength. So if your gut feels
off... always listen. Your gut is your superpower. Yeah... that's true.

Because out here, pretty is optional. Polite is optional. Alive is the whole
assignment. Stay sexy. Stay feral. Stay alive.
