# 07 · Publishing

## Where this format lives

| Platform | Format | Why |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | primary | The format was born here; comments do the distribution work |
| Instagram Reels | same file, no changes | Second-highest yield for zero extra effort |
| YouTube Shorts | same file | Slowest to start, best long-tail and best RPM |
| Facebook Reels | same file | Genuinely large audience for this exact comedy register in the US |

One render, four uploads. Do not make platform-specific cuts until one platform is
clearly working.

## Titles

The title, the top caption and the first spoken line all point at the same thing. Keep
them identical or near-identical:

```
caption:  Eating a Chipotle bowl right now:
title:    Eating a Chipotle bowl right now 🫠
```

Rules:
- Name the brand or the food in the first four words.
- No "POV" unless the episode genuinely is a POV.
- One emoji maximum, at the end, and only a face or a body-adjacent one.
- Never put the punchline in the title.

## Hashtags

Three to five. Two broad, two specific, one brand/food:

```
#foodtok #relatable #chipotle #digestion #cartoon
```

Do not chase unrelated trending tags. This format's audience finds it through the food
term, and the food term is the one that matters.

## Cadence

- **3 per week minimum** to establish the format. Viewers need to see the cast three
  times before "the stomach guy" becomes a thing they recognize.
- **Batch production.** Write six episodes in one sitting, generate all six on one
  afternoon, schedule across two weeks. The assets are already made; the marginal cost of
  episode seven is six clips.
- **Seasonal episodes are scheduled backwards** — Thanksgiving posts the Monday of that
  week, Super Bowl the Friday before, state fair in August.

## The comment engine

This format's real distribution is the comment section, and comments come from
disagreement about the food, not about the video. Design for it:

- **Pick foods people defend.** Chipotle portions, pineapple on pizza, ranch on
  everything, sweet tea, well-done steak. A food nobody has an opinion about is a dead
  episode regardless of how good the writing is.
- **Leave one thing unresolved.** The reference never explains what was actually wrong
  with the greens. Viewers argue about it in the comments. Do that on purpose.
- **Reply in character.** Answer top comments as Gut. It costs nothing and it is the
  single strongest retention move available to a faceless channel.
- **Take requests.** "Do pre-workout next" is a free content calendar and it makes the
  requester an evangelist for that episode.

## Retention structure

The format is already built for it, but check each episode against this:

| Time | What must be true |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | A character is already mid-argument. No logo, no intro, no title card. |
| 0:00–0:09 | The food is named, out loud and in the caption. |
| 0:30 | Something new has entered the frame — the food has a face by now. |
| 0:45 | A new character has appeared who was not in the first half. |
| last 3 s | The kicker, flat, and then cut. No outro, no "follow for more". |

**No end card.** The last frame of the video is the last frame of the joke. An end card
is a two-second retention drop on a sixty-second video, on every single upload, forever.

## Monetization reality check

- **YouTube Shorts** pays on the RPM pool; a US-heavy audience on food content is a
  decent RPM. Advertiser-friendliness is why `02-americanization.md` caps the language.
- **TikTok** pays essentially nothing directly at this length. It is the audience
  builder, and it is where brand deals come from.
- **The actual money in this format is food brand integrations** — a chain paying to be
  the guest food and to *not* get destroyed. That deal only exists if the channel has
  never posted anything a brand safety team would flag. Every guardrail in `02` is a
  monetization decision.
- **Disclose sponsorships** per FTC guidance, in the caption and on-platform, every time.

## What to measure

Per episode, log four numbers:

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| 3-second retention | Is the cold open working |
| Average watch % | Is the middle sagging (usually block 4) |
| Comments ÷ views | Did the food choice provoke |
| Shares ÷ views | Is it relatable enough to send to a friend |

Shares is the one that predicts the next video's reach. Comments is the one that predicts
whether the food vein has more episodes in it.
