# The Wipe Out — Performance Script

**Runtime:** ~2:10 spoken
**Piece:** first-person storytelling monologue
**Voice:** conversational at the top, tightening to almost-whisper in the middle, flat and honest at the end

**Delivery key:**
`/` short breath · `//` full stop, hold 2 seconds · **bold** = land the word · *(italics)* = stage/tone note

---

## PART 1 — EASY (open loose, like you're telling a friend)

That morning, / I thought it was going to be a normal day. //

It was July 5th. /
The fireworks were over. /
Everything still smelled like smoke and sunscreen.

Me and my friends went down to the Jersey Shore. /
Walked the boardwalk. / Got food. /
Nothing about that day was supposed to be a story.

*(small smile — you're still in the good part)*

And then we ended up at Playland's Castaway Cove. //

And we looked up at the **Wipe Out**.

---

## PART 2 — CONFIDENT (this is the part you'll regret; play it light)

*(shrug in the voice)*

I've been on rides like this before. /
So I wasn't nervous. / Not even a little.

We strapped in. /
The bar came down. /
I remember I didn't even check it. //

*(pick up pace here — let the ride build)*

It started swinging. / Higher. / Higher. /
Sky, / ocean, / sky, / ocean —
everyone screaming, / everyone laughing. //

*(pace peaks — then cut it dead)*

And then —

## `[ FULL STOP — hold 3 seconds. Don't rush this. ]`

it stopped. //

---

## PART 3 — THE HANG (slow way down. Present tense. Quiet.)

*(from here, drop your volume — make them lean in)*

At first / I think it's part of the ride. /
They do that, right? / They hold you. / For the drama.

So I'm waiting. //

*(beat)*

And I'm still waiting. //

Then I look around. //

And I notice the ocean is / **above** / my head.

*(beat)*

We're upside down. /
And we're **not moving**. //

*(name the small physical details — this is where it gets real)*

You start noticing things you never notice. /
Your shirt hanging over your face. /
The blood in your ears. /
Your phone in your pocket, / one wrong second from the ground. /
The laughing turning into something / that is not laughing anymore.

*(slowest line in the piece — separate every word)*

And then I looked down at the restraint across my lap /
and I had one thought. //

*(quiet. barely voiced. this is the center of the whole piece)*

**"What if this thing opens?"** //

## `[ HOLD 3 SECONDS. Let it sit. ]`

*(flat, factual — the contrast does the work)*

We stayed like that for several minutes. //

Minutes. /
Upside down. /
Doing math on a metal bar.

---

## PART 4 — THE RELEASE (sharp, sudden — jolt them)

Then the ride **moved**. //

*(loud — one burst)*

And everyone screamed. //

*(immediately back down to nothing)*

But this time / nobody was having fun. //

---

## PART 5 — THE BUTTON (no performance left. Just tell the truth.)

*(drop the storytelling voice completely — talk like you're on the boardwalk again)*

When we finally got off, / my legs didn't work right. /
Everybody did that thing where you laugh /
because you don't know what else to do with your face. //

And I just stood there. /
Looking up at it. /
Watching it load the next group. //

## `[ Beat. Look up. ]`

*(final line: quiet, certain, no joke in it)*

I am **never** riding that thing again. //

*(hold. then out.)*

# PERFORMANCE NOTES

**The one rule:** the whole piece lives or dies on the silence after *"it stopped."* Count it out — three real seconds. It will feel far too long to you and exactly right to them.

**Three speeds, in order:**
1. Casual (Parts 1–2) — you're bored, this is a nothing story
2. Slow and quiet (Part 3) — you're back inside it
3. Plain (Part 5) — you're not performing anymore, you're just saying it

**Volume shape:** medium → loud (the swinging) → near-whisper (upside down) → one loud burst (the scream) → quiet (the button). Never louder than that one burst.

**Hands:** keep them still through Parts 1–2. The only gesture you need is looking *up* at the end.

**Do not** smile on the last line, and do not add anything after it. The laugh you'd get from a joke there costs you the ending.
