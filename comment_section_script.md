## PRODUCTION NOTES

| Element | Note |
|---|---|
| Pacing | ~5–7s per comment. If a comment is one word, spend 2s. Variety is the rhythm. |
| Comment #9 | Hold the longest. It's the payoff and it gets called back at the end. |
| The sigh | Isolate the audio. Boost it slightly. Do not narrate over it — ever. |
| Reactions | One line each, max. The moment you explain a joke you kill it. |
| Music | Low, warm, nothing with vocals. Duck it fully under the clip audio. |
| Captions | Burn in every comment verbatim, typos included. The typos are part of it. |
| Retention hook | "Okay, this comment section is so funny" in the first 2 seconds, before anything else. |
| Ending | No "like and subscribe." "Show it to your friends" *is* the CTA. |
