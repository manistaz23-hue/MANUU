# "Thank God (And Absolutely No One Else)" — Skit Scenario Bank

Working premise: someone does heroic, visible, exhausting work to save a
person — and the person walks straight past them to thank God / the pastor.
The comedy engine is **misplaced credit**: the actual helper is always in
frame, ignored, often still bleeding/sweating/on fire. Play the thanker as
100% sincere and the helper as deadpan-invisible.

Unifying meta-gag: God runs a **Cosmic Credit Department** — he claims every
win ("I'll take that one") and routes every loss to "Mysterious Ways &
Free Will LLC." Wins: God. Losses: not God. That's the whole business model.

## 1. The Surgery Testimony (your anchor bit)

**Setup:** Church service. Patient at the pulpit, fresh scar, tears of joy.
The surgeon — still in scrubs, eye bags down to her chin, 9 hours of
operating behind her — sits quietly in the third pew.

> PATIENT: "The doctors said it was risky! But I want to thank the REAL
> healer... Pastor Mike and the LORD!"
> *(congregation erupts; surgeon slow-blinks)*
> PASTOR MIKE (who visited once, for five minutes, ate the get-well fruit
> basket): "All glory to God. And a small love offering to me."
> SURGEON (to camera, monotone): "I rerouted his aorta with my hands."
> PATIENT: "She was God's INSTRUMENT!"
> SURGEON: "Instruments get sterilized and thanked. I got street parking."

**Button:** God upstairs, stamping a form: "One (1) miracle — CREDIT: ME.
Labor: 14 years of med school, not mine, don't care. APPROVED."

## 2. The Sole Survivor

**Setup:** News interview at a crash site. One survivor wrapped in a blanket.

> SURVIVOR: "God saved me! He wrapped his hands around me!"
> REPORTER: "There were 200 other passengers."
> SURVIVOR: "God is good!"
> REPORTER: "So... he was on the plane... and chose... just you."
> SURVIVOR: "His favorite!"
> *(cut to God upstairs looking at the passenger manifest like a menu)*
> GOD: "Seat 23C. He seemed fun. The rest — mysterious ways."
> GABRIEL (HR angel): "Sir, you can't keep saying that in reports."

## 3. The Flood Joke, Weaponized (the classic, but the boats get lines)

**Setup:** Man on his roof, floodwater rising. Rescue boat pulls up.

> MAN: "No thanks! God will save me!"
> BOAT GUY: "Sir, I'm literally—"
> MAN: "GOD. WILL. SAVE ME."
> *(second boat. Helicopter. Jet ski. A dolphin. He waves them ALL off.)*
> *(Man arrives in heaven, furious)*
> MAN: "Lord, why didn't you save me?!"
> GOD: "I sent two boats, a helicopter, a jet ski, and a dolphin. The
> dolphin wasn't even on shift. He volunteered."

## 4. Fire Department vs. Prayer Department

**Setup:** House fire. Firefighter carries out a whole family, one by one,
then goes BACK IN for the cat. Emerges smoking, eyebrows gone.

> MOM (on the news): "The Lord shielded this family with his wings!"
> FIREFIGHTER (in background, extinguishing his own boot): "..."
> REPORTER: "Any thanks to the fire department?"
> MOM: "They were there, yes. God works THROUGH people."
> FIREFIGHTER: "He can work through my hospital bill too then."

## 6. Grace Before Dinner (Supply Chain Edition)

**Setup:** Family says grace. As they pray, the camera walks the food's
actual journey in reverse — cook, delivery driver, warehouse worker,

> DAD: "Thank you Lord for providing this meal."
> *(hard cut: the farmer, mid-downpour, alone, staring into camera)*
> FARMER: "Provided by WHO?"
> *(cut back)*
> DAD: "...and bless the hands that prepared it."
> COOK (from kitchen): "NAME! SAY MY NAME! IT'S DENISE!"

## 10. Act of God (The Insurance Loophole)

**Setup:** The ONE institution that officially credits God — and only for
destruction. Insurance office.

> AGENT: "Tornado took your roof? That's an Act of God. Not covered."
> MAN: "But when God SAVED my house from the last tornado—"
> AGENT: "That was luck. Not covered either."
> MAN: "So God only officially exists when you don't have to pay?"
> AGENT: "Now you understand insurance. AND theology."
> *(cut to God reading the policy)* GOD: "They blame me in WRITING? Even I
> route my losses through Mysterious Ways LLC."

> GABRIEL: "Tornadoes don't HAVE free will!"

## Runners / Recurring Bits

- **The Credit Stamp:** every skit ends in God's office — one form stamped
  "CREDIT: GOD ✅", one stamped "BLAME: MYSTERIOUS WAYS ↩️". By the finale
  the Mysterious Ways outbox is a mountain.
- **Gabriel the Compliance Angel:** keeps trying to get God to acknowledge
  "human contributors" in the quarterly miracle report. God keeps writing
  "instruments (uncredited)."
- **The Ignored Helper Stare:** every human helper gets one silent, direct
  4th-wall look into camera. Same zoom, same sting sound. It becomes the
  signature of the series.
- **Biscuit the K-9:** returns in later episodes. Still uncredited. Still
  knows.
- **The Point-at-the-Sky:** anyone doing anything successful points up.
  A barista makes decent foam — points up. God accepts all of them.

## Tone Notes

- The joke targets the **hypocrisy and credit theft**, not faith itself —
  the thankers are lovable and sincere, which is exactly what makes it
  funny. Never make them villains; make them oblivious.
- The helpers should underplay. The angrier the situation deserves, the
  flatter their delivery. Deadpan is the whole sauce.
- God plays like a friendly middle manager gaming the metrics: cheerfully
  claims wins, expertly deflects losses, zero self-awareness.
- Reference material for tone: George Carlin ("Religion is Bullsh*t" —
  especially the 'thank God / blame no one' logic), Ricky Gervais on
  answered prayers, sole-survivor news-interview parodies, and the classic
  "two boats and a helicopter" joke (skit #3 is that joke with the boats
  given speaking roles).
