# "Get You Pregnant" — 1-Minute Bit

**Runtime target:** ~60 seconds
**Tone:** fast, deadpan-serious escalating rant, comedic

---

Ladies, listen to me very carefully. When a man looks you dead in your eyes
and says, "I wanna get you pregnant" — do you know what you should
automatically hear, subconsciously? You should hear: "I want to unalive you.
I want to annihilate your entire family. Grandma? Gone. The group chat?
Deleted. Then I'm going into all of your bank accounts — checking, savings,
even the secret one with forty-seven dollars in it. Your Social Security?
Mine now. I'm stealing your whole identity. I'm becoming you. I'm wearing
your bonnet. I'm answering your texts. I'm living your whole life — badly —
ruining everything you ever worked for." And just when you think it's over?
He revives you from the dead... just to unalive you again. Twice. On a
Tuesday. So no, sir, I don't hear romance. I don't hear baby names. I hear
a whole federal case. "I wanna get you pregnant"? Baby, that's not a
compliment — that's a confession.
